# Monetization Model

> All figures are in **Toman** (1 Toman = 10 Rial) — see [[Glossary]]. Current numbers are AI-proposed and accepted as a working baseline, **not yet validated** with real trainers or users — see [[Validation Plan]].

## Subscription
| Plan | Price | Daily scans |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Free | — | contested: 2 or 5 — see [[Known Inconsistencies]] #1 |
| Pro | 490,000 Toman/month | 15 |

⚠️ At an estimated ~1,780 Toman cost per AI scan, 15 scans/day ≈ 801,000 Toman/month in API cost alone — **above** the 490,000 price if a user maxes out the cap every day. Break-even is roughly 9 scans/day. This needs real usage data before it's trustworthy — see [[Known Inconsistencies]] #3.

## Coins (non-subscriber pay-per-scan)
| Package | Scans | Price |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Small | 5 | 25,000 Toman |
| Medium | 12 | 55,000 Toman |
| Large | 25 | 100,000 Toman |

Coins do not expire.

## Commissions
| Source | Rate |
|--------|------|
| Trainer booking | 10% of the user's payment |
| Gym membership fee | 5% of the user's payment |

Whether this is deducted from the trainer/gym payout or added on top of the user's price is unresolved — see [[Open Questions]] #1.

## Elite
Earned (6 months + trainer approval), not purchased — see [[Levels Ranking and Elite Status]].

## Pricing history (for context, not current)
First proposal was 245,000 Toman/month with 5 scans/day — revised upward after a cost sanity-check. See [[Known Inconsistencies]] #7.
