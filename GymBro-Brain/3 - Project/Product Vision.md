# Product Vision

## One-liner
GymBro (gymbros.com) is a **Persian-first fitness marketplace** built on Uber's supply-demand model: trainers replace drivers, users replace riders, and gyms are a second supply side.

## The problem
- Trainers currently work through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp — manual, unstructured, no shared history
- Users struggle to find a trustworthy trainer or gym; there's no transparent discovery layer
- Workout plans are still PDFs or paper — not interactive, no reliable visual demonstration of correct form (injury risk)
- Calorie and nutrition tracking is manual and tedious — most people abandon it
- Global apps (e.g. MyFitnessPal) aren't built for Iran: not Persian, not Toman-priced, not filtering-resilient

## The solution
- Digital, interactive workout plans with exercise demo videos (not paper/PDF)
- AI calorie scanning from a food photo
- Automatic conversion of a gym plan into a home workout when the user can't make it to the gym
- Nutrition plans linked to the calorie scanner, with automatic over-limit alerts to both user and trainer
- GPS-based gym discovery, in-app trainer booking, and in-app gym-fee payment
- A status/ranking system (member → athlete → pro → elite) for motivation and social trust

## Why trainers would pay commission when Instagram is free
Three real reasons, surfaced directly in a "does GymBro build real value?" discussion:
1. **Discovery** — trainers meet customers they'd never have reached otherwise (this benefit grows with the user base, so it's weak in the very first cohort of trainers)
2. **Reduced support load** — a clean template + demo videos means fewer repeated explanations to clients
3. **Data lock-in** — once a trainer's full client history and plans live in GymBro, going back to WhatsApp means losing all of that organization

For the first 12–15 trainers (personal network), reason 2 and 3 are the honest pitch — reason 1 isn't real yet at that stage.
