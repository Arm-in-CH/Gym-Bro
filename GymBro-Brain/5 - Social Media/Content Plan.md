# Content Plan

## Platform sequencing
**Instagram first, YouTube later.**
- Instagram: fast to start, low production overhead, reels fit fitness content naturally, matches the Iranian fitness audience
- YouTube: deferred — higher production cost, 6–12 months to build real traction, better suited once there's a real user base and deeper stories to tell

## Rule of thumb
Don't start content before the product exists to talk about — build-in-public content (see [[Personal Brand Strategy]]) should document a real, in-progress product, not an idea with nothing behind it yet.
