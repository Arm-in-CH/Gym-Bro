# Video Production Setup

## Camera
**Sony A6400** — purchased for GymBro personal-brand content.
- Strong autofocus (important for movement-heavy workout footage)
- Clean 4K without an intrusive crop
- Light and portable enough to bring to the gym
- Flip screen — useful for solo/selfie-style filming

## Studio setup
Talking-head content recorded in a controlled space with **softbox lighting**. In controlled light, the A6400's low-light limitations stop mattering — autofocus and 4K quality are what matter most for a "talking to camera" format.

## Practical notes
- For sessions over ~30–40 minutes, run on external power rather than battery alone (heat/battery life)
- The built-in mic isn't sufficient for studio-quality audio — a shotgun or lavalier mic is needed
