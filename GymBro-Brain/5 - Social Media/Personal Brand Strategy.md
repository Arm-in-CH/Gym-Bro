# Personal Brand Strategy

## The core idea
Armin isn't just a founder who happens to run a fitness app — he's a **real gym-goer actively improving his own physique**, which is GymBro's actual target audience. He wants to be recognized on the gym side specifically for this, not just as "a CEO."

## Why this is stronger than generic founder content
Authenticity that can't be bought with ads: a CEO/backend developer who visibly lives the product. When GymBro eventually launches, a founder users already recognize as one of their own is a strong trust signal.

## Content mix that was agreed on
| Content | Signals |
|---------|---------|
| Armin's own training / physique progress | Credibility — he's a real user, not just an operator |
| Build-in-public: code, bugs, technical decisions | "Built for people like me" |
| CEO perspective on why Iran fitness needs this | Vision, not just product |
| Combined posts (e.g. "did deadlifts, then shipped this feature") | The two halves of his identity reinforcing each other |

## Sequencing
Start **before** the app is fully built — "build in public" — rather than waiting for launch. People engage with the journey, not just the destination.
