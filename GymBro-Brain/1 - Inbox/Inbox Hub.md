# 1 - Inbox

The capture zone: unresolved questions, contradictions, and half-formed ideas. Anything here is **not settled** — do not treat it as a decision.

| Note | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| [[Open Questions]] | Decisions and facts still needed |
| [[Known Inconsistencies]] | Places where earlier statements or exported files disagree |
| [[Ideas Parking Lot]] | Future ideas deliberately kept off the current roadmap |

## Triage rule
When an item is resolved, move the outcome into the right note (usually [[Architecture Decisions]] or a feature note), then strike it here.
