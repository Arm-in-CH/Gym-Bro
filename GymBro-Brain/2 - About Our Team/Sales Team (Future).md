# Sales Team (Future — Phase 2/3)

Not hired yet. Planned once the personal-network trainer pipeline is exhausted — see [[Trainer Partnership Program]].

## Role: `sales_agent`
- New user role, alongside `user` / `trainer` / `gym_owner` / `admin`
- Cold-calls and onboards **gyms and trainers**, modeled on Snapp's business-development approach
- Works the admin-panel [[CRM and Sales Pipeline]]: `leads` and `lead_activities` tables
- Scoped access: sees only `leads` and `lead_activities`, not financial or regular user data (enforced via RBAC/PBAC)

## Why it exists
The first 12–15 trainers come from Armin's own network. Beyond that, someone has to do outbound outreach the way Snapp's BD team recruits drivers and restaurants — this role is that function, formalized.
