# ML Lead — Food Recognition Model

Armin's cousin (his maternal uncle's daughter): teaches machine learning, holds a PhD in AI, and is a member of the GymBro team.

## Role
Building the **in-house Iranian food-recognition model** — the long-term replacement for the GPT-4o Vision calls used in [[Calorie Scanning and Nutrition]].

## Status
- Contributing unpaid for now (no formal written agreement yet — see [[Open Questions]] #23)
- Armin floated a budget of roughly **40 million Toman** for someone to do food-data entry (photographing/labelling varied dishes to train the model)
- Every calorie-scan photo and AI response is logged to MongoDB specifically to build this future training dataset — see [[AI Architecture]]

## Why this matters strategically
Global vision models are weak on Persian/Iranian dishes. A proprietary, Iran-tuned model is a real moat once enough scan data accumulates.
