# Validation Plan

## What hasn't been tested
- Pricing (490,000 Toman Pro, 10%/5% commissions) — never checked against a real trainer's or gym owner's willingness to pay
- Whether trainers currently using Instagram/WhatsApp actually feel the pain points GymBro assumes
- Free-scan allowance and its real cost impact — see [[Known Inconsistencies]] #1 and #3
- Whether the "discovery" value prop lands with the very first trainer cohort (it structurally can't, with zero existing users — see [[Trainer Partnership Program]])

## Recommended first step
Talk to 5–10 real trainers before finishing the build — specifically to find out whether they actually have the problem GymBro assumes, not to pitch the solution. (*The Mom Test* — see [[Reading List]] — was recommended specifically for this kind of conversation.)
