# Founder Reading List

A 16-book, quarter-by-quarter list compiled for Armin's development as a founder, organized around GymBro's actual stage-by-stage needs.

## Q1 — Startup and product fundamentals
1. *The Lean Startup* — Eric Ries
2. *Zero to One* — Peter Thiel
3. *The Cold Start Problem* — Andrew Chen (directly informed [[Go-To-Market Strategy]])
4. *Inspired* — Marty Cagan

## Q2 — Marketing and growth
5. *Traction* — Gabriel Weinberg
6. *Building a StoryBrand* — Donald Miller
7. *Contagious* — Jonah Berger
8. *$100M Offers* — Alex Hormozi

## Q3 — Sales and user acquisition
9. *Never Split the Difference* — Chris Voss
10. *The Mom Test* — Rob Fitzpatrick (see [[Validation Plan]])
11. *Hooked* — Nir Eyal (informed [[Levels Ranking and Elite Status]])
12. *$100M Leads* — Alex Hormozi

## Q4 — Leadership and scale
13. *The Hard Thing About Hard Things* — Ben Horowitz
14. *Blitzscaling* — Reid Hoffman
15. *Good to Great* — Jim Collins
16. *Platform Revolution* — Parker / Van Alstyne / Choudary

## If time is short, read these four first
*The Cold Start Problem* → *$100M Offers* → *Hooked* → *The Mom Test*
