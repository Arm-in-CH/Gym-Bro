# Positioning and Differentiation

## Vs. Instagram/WhatsApp coaching (the real current alternative)
Most Iranian trainers today coach informally over DMs. GymBro's pitch isn't "cheaper" — it's:
- A clean, structured plan format instead of scattered messages
- Exercise demo videos instead of text-only instructions
- All client history retained in one place (a real switching cost, in GymBro's favor once adopted)

## Vs. global fitness apps (e.g. MyFitnessPal)
- Persian-first UI and Toman pricing
- Built to route around Iran's internet filtering from day one (OpenRouter, DeepSeek) rather than retrofitted
- Local trainer/gym supply network, not just a generic tracking tool

## Honest caveat
No formal competitive research into local Iranian apps has been done — see [[Competitive Landscape]] and [[Validation Plan]]. These differentiation claims are reasoned, not yet market-tested.
